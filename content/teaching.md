---

## Introduction to Computer Vision

*Graduate — University of Houston*

Computer vision is transforming how machines perceive and interpret the visual world — from autonomous vehicles to medical diagnostics. This course provides a comprehensive introduction to the fundamental algorithms and techniques that enable computers to extract meaningful information from images and video. Topics include image formation and filtering, feature detection and description, geometric transformations, object recognition, and an introduction to deep learning for visual understanding. Students gain hands-on experience implementing and evaluating vision algorithms on real-world problems, building a strong foundation for further study in machine learning and AI.

---

## Advanced Deep Learning for Image Analysis

*Graduate — University of Houston*

This advanced course explores the cutting edge of deep learning with a focus on image analysis under challenging real-world conditions. Topics include deep learning for multi-channel and multi-sensor imagery, learning with limited labeled data, generative adversarial networks (GANs) for data augmentation, vision transformers, self-supervised learning, transfer learning, and meta-learning. Applications are drawn from geospatial imaging (GeoAI) and biomedicine, where students work on hands-on projects with real datasets provided by the instructor. Students are also expected to read and present recent research papers, developing the skills needed to engage with the latest advances in the field.

---

## Sparse Representations for Signal Processing

*Graduate — University of Houston*

Sparse representations are a cornerstone of modern signal processing, with applications spanning denoising, compression, compressive sensing, and machine learning across speech, image, and video signals. This course covers the mathematical foundations of sparse signal representations through multi-resolution analysis and wavelet theory, as well as data-learned representations used in deep neural networks. Students explore the interplay between structured sparsity and deep learning, including emerging methods for designing efficient neural networks. The course combines theoretical rigor with a hands-on project component in which students apply sparsity-based techniques to a real-world image analysis problem.

---

## Stochastic Processes in Signal Processing and Data Science

*Graduate — University of Houston*

A solid understanding of probability and stochastic processes underpins virtually every modern data science and machine learning method. This course provides a thorough treatment of probability theory, random variables, and stochastic processes, linking these foundations directly to practical data science applications such as Bayesian inference, Bayesian classification, subspace learning, clustering, and denoising. Students develop both theoretical understanding and practical skills through Python-based projects, applying the concepts learned in class to real-world signal processing and data analysis problems.
