I am a Full Professor in the [Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://ece.egr.uh.edu/) at the [University of Houston](https://www.uh.edu/), where I direct the **Machine Learning and Signal Processing (MLSP) Laboratory**.

My research advances machine learning and signal processing for **geospatial imaging (GeoAI)** and **biomedicine**. Current focus areas include foundation models, parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA), active learning with human-in-the-loop workflows, and multi-sensor image analysis. Lab supported by NASA, NSF, NIH, DoD, and Amazon AWS. I am an **IEEE GRSS Distinguished Lecturer** and Senior Member of IEEE. I organize [GeoCV](https://sites.google.com/view/geocv/) at WACV and [MORSE](https://sites.google.com/view/cvpr-morse) at CVPR.
