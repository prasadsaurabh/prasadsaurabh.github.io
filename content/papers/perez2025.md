##### Abstract

We present a layer-optimized spatial-spectral masked autoencoder for semantic segmentation of hyperspectral imagery, leveraging vision transformer architectures with spectral-spatial attention mechanisms. The layer optimization strategy selects the most informative transformer layers for downstream segmentation tasks.
