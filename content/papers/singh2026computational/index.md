##### Abstract

This paper presents a computational framework for the delineation and cellular profiling of murine cortical cell layers using multiplex immunofluorescence (IF) microscopy. The approach leverages deep learning and image analysis methods to automatically identify and characterize distinct cell populations across cortical layers, providing quantitative profiling of cell types, spatial distributions, and molecular marker expressions. The framework supports large-scale brain tissue analysis and enables systematic comparison across experimental conditions.
