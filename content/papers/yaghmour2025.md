##### Abstract

Going beyond simple fine-tuning: knowledge transfer in the context of geospatial foundation models. This work addresses domain differences that arise under variations in sensors and sensing conditions, which can otherwise lead to sub-optimal performance when deploying foundation models. We propose a framework combining pseudo-labeling and generative learning for robust cross-sensor knowledge transfer.
