##### Abstract

We present an end-to-end framework for adapting Large Vision Models (LVMs) to geospatial semantic segmentation tasks. Our acquisition function combines traditional uncertainty measures with model gradients to identify pixels that will most effectively improve the model's performance. The approach outperforms existing methods across various labeling budgets when transferring models to previously unseen global regions.
