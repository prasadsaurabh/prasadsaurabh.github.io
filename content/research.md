## Machine Learning and Signal Processing Laboratory

The MLSP Laboratory at the University of Houston advances state-of-the-art machine learning and signal processing with applications to geospatial imaging (GeoAI) and biomedicine. Our lab is supported by **NASA, NSF, NIH, DoD, and Amazon AWS**.

---

## GeoAI — Geospatial Image Analysis

Modern geospatial imaging via active and passive sensing modalities (multispectral, hyperspectral, LiDAR, SAR) aboard satellites and aircraft presents remarkable opportunities and significant challenges.

**Current research directions:**
- Foundation models and large vision models for geospatial semantic segmentation
- Parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA) of vision transformers for earth observation
- Active learning and human-in-the-loop workflows for annotation efficiency
- Sensor-agnostic domain generalization across imaging conditions
- Multi-sensor, multi-scale image analysis

**Workshops organized:**
- [GeoCV](https://sites.google.com/view/geocv/) at WACV — Geospatial Image Analysis (2025, 2026)
- [MORSE](https://sites.google.com/view/cvpr-morse) at CVPR — Foundation Models in Remote Sensing (2025, 2026)

**Sponsors:** NASA, NSF, DoD, Amazon AWS

## AI in Biomedicine

Modern biomedical imaging systems (e.g., FTIR microscopy and highly-multiplexed immunofluorescence microscopy) capture very high resolution imagery with rich multi-channel characterization of underlying biological processes.

**Current research directions:**
- Cell phenotype identification in multiplexed immunofluorescence microscopy
- Visual prompting and incremental learning for biomedical segmentation
- Spatial proteomics and tissue atlas construction
- Deep learning for FTIR spectral analysis

**Sponsors:** NIH, DoD
