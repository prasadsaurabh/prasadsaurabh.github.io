The MLSP Lab is recruiting PhD students in GeoAI and machine learning.

## Research areas

- Foundation models and large vision models
- Active learning and human-in-the-loop AI
- Multi-sensor geospatial image analysis
- Biomedical image analysis

See [Research](/research/) for current projects and [Papers](/papers/) for recent work.

## Who we're looking for

Motivated students with strong backgrounds in electrical engineering, computer science, or related fields. Ideal candidates have experience with deep learning frameworks (PyTorch/TensorFlow) and a passion for research in GeoAI or biomedical image analysis.

## How to apply

1. **Fill out the [interest form](https://forms.cloud.microsoft/r/XTFj5Z30nx).** Include links to your CV, transcripts, and any published work (Google Scholar, arXiv, or similar).
2. **Apply to the UH ECE PhD program.** See the department's [graduate admission guidelines](https://www.ece.uh.edu/graduate/admission-guidelines).
3. **Questions?** Email [sprasad2@uh.edu](mailto:sprasad2@uh.edu).

## Before you start

My [Research Student Guide](/research-guide/) is a 55-slide guide to thesis and dissertation work at UH ECE: the research mindset, running experiments, writing, ethics and collaboration, presenting, and weekly routines.
